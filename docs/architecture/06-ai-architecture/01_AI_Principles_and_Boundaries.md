# AI Principles and Boundaries

**Document ID:** PS-AI-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved

## Principles

1. AI is non-authoritative.
2. AI receives bounded context.
3. AI outputs are untrusted until validated.
4. Prompts, personas, and schemas are versioned.
5. Material outputs retain provenance.
6. AI failure must not corrupt or block core simulation state.
7. The learner retains agency.
8. Hidden scenario logic must not be leaked.
9. Sensitive data is minimized.
10. Deterministic rules remain outside AI.

## Authority Boundary

AI may:

- Explain a decision outcome
- Suggest reflection questions
- Generate stakeholder wording
- Summarize a project situation
- Recommend learning activities

AI may not:

- Mark a Day complete
- Change mastery directly
- Alter trust scores directly
- Resolve a decision outside the domain engine
- Change project metrics directly
- Publish authoritative events
- Bypass authorization

## Typed Action Boundary

AI-proposed changes must become typed commands and pass normal domain validation.
