## 2. Catalog Summary

Lead a nine-month, $4.8 million transformation to improve patient access across a 12-clinic community health network. Evaluate evidence, align competing stakeholders, tailor the delivery approach, manage a major vendor and integration effort, respond to a crisis, and guide the program toward a defensible outcome.

## 5. Supported Experience Levels

### Explorer

For learners with little or no formal project-management experience.

Includes terminology support, guided evidence prompts, optional hints, and stronger mentor guidance.

### Practitioner

For learners with working project-management knowledge.

Includes realistic ambiguity, limited hints, competing expectations, and moderate pressure.

### Leader

For experienced practitioners.

Includes minimal prompting, higher ambiguity, tighter trade-offs, and optional advanced governance and procurement challenges.
