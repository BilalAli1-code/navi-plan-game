## PMP Candidate
Needs PMBOK-aligned practice, decision explanations, and readiness indicators.

## New Project Manager
Needs realistic workplace practice, coaching, reflection, and replayable scenarios.

## Experienced Project Manager
Needs advanced scenarios, nuanced stakeholder behavior, and detailed analytics.

## Instructor
Needs assignment controls, cohort reporting, learner reports, and evidence visibility.

## Enterprise Learning Manager
Needs organization controls, privacy, benchmarks, exports, and integrations.

## Content Author
Needs schemas, validation, preview, testing, and versioning.
