# Success Metrics

**Document ID:** PS-PROD-006  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved

## Learning
Competency improvement, assessment improvement, reflection completion, evidence coverage, exam readiness, and confidence calibration.

## Engagement
Simulation starts, Day completion, full completion, replay, session duration, and return frequency.

## Product Quality
Action failure rate, duplicate-effect rate, projection consistency, AI validation acceptance, accessibility conformance, and support incidents.

## Suggested Pilot Indicators

- At least 70% of activated learners complete Day 1.
- At least 50% complete the full MVP simulation.
- Less than 1% of accepted actions produce processing errors.
- Zero duplicate mastery or metric effects from retries.
- At least 80% report improved confidence.
