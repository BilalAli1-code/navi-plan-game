# Product Vision

**Document ID:** PS-PROD-001  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Approved  
**Owner:** Product Management

## Executive Summary

ProjectSim 2.0 is an AI-powered project leadership simulation platform designed to transform project management education from passive study into practical experience.

## Vision Statement

Become the leading experiential platform for project leadership development by enabling professionals to learn through realistic practice rather than memorization.

## Mission

Help project professionals develop judgment, confidence, communication, and leadership through immersive simulations, intelligent coaching, and evidence-based learning analytics.

## Product Pillars

1. Realistic simulation
2. Consequence-driven decisions
3. Persistent stakeholder relationships
4. Evidence-based learning
5. AI-assisted coaching
6. Replayability
7. Explainable outcomes
8. Content extensibility
