# BC-007 — Learner Workload and Assessment Review

## 1. Approved workload standard

From Simulation Design Blueprint / Full Simulation Blueprint / package manifest:

- **10 learning days**
- **~60 minutes/day**
- **600 estimated minutes** total
- Flexible pace; no calendar unlock

## 2. Estimation methodology (deterministic, not measured)

Per chapter:

`estimatedMinutes = requiredDecisions×8 + requiredActivities×4 + meetings×6 + documents×3 + messages×2`

This distinguishes required decision/activity burden from supporting evidence volume. It is **not** a measured learner study result.

## 3. Workload findings

- Package manifest remains `estimatedMinutes: 600`, `learningDays: 10`.
- Deterministic chapter estimates sum to a band inside 300–1200 minutes (audit assertion).
- Chapter Six carries the heaviest required decision/activity load (closure + benefits), consistent with blueprint emphasis on closure learning.
- No chapter added; no new major decisions invented to “balance” workload.
- Residual risk: document boilerplate still contributes reading volume; full prose differentiation deferred as non-blocking (BC007-WORKLOAD-001 accepted limitation).
